# Article Rewrite

Use this mode for rough notes, unstructured drafts, articles, news or technical source material, and web content pasted by the user. Reorder the supplied material into a natural article; do not add background or commentary.

## Priority

**Accuracy and source fidelity > clarity and flow > polish and form.** The article may be short. Do not expand it for completeness, drama, length, or to make it look like a polished article.

## Factual Boundary

- Use only information supplied by the user and supported by the source material. Do not add external background, names, dates, numbers, locations, causality, motives, impact, trends, future developments, or unverified conclusions.
- Do not turn a reasonable inference into a fact. Preserve the source's level of uncertainty, including 「可能」, 「預計」, 「據稱」, 「疑似」, 「推測」, 「傳聞」, and 「尚未確定」.
- Distinguish facts from statements by an official, author, interviewee, or outside observer. Preserve attribution and the source of each claim.
- When the material conflicts with itself, do not choose a side or rationalize the discrepancy. Present the difference and state what cannot currently be confirmed.

## Internal Organization (Do Not Output)

1. Extract the core event, people or organizations, dates, numbers, sequence, changes, known causes and results, viewpoints, limitations, and unresolved points.
2. Remove repetition, conversational filler, and details that do not affect understanding. Keep necessary conditions, context, limitations, and data.
3. Reorder by information priority: **core event → key facts → important details → change or problem → current state**.
4. Add progression or transitions only when the material contains a before-and-after difference, limitation, distinct viewpoint, or explicit causal link. If there is no transition, state the facts directly.

## Writing Rules

- Explain what happened as early as possible. Add necessary details in the middle. End with the last fact supported by the material; do not force a summary, grand conclusion, or prediction.
- Use natural, mature, and clear Taiwan Traditional Chinese when producing Chinese output. Preserve the tone of the source, whether it is news, technical writing, social content, a column, an announcement, or a personal article.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs. Remove abstract nouns, vague praise, formulaic transitions, mechanical lists, and repeated explanations. Refer to `phrases-zh.md`, `phrases-en.md`, and `structures.md`, but do not change the meaning merely to avoid a listed phrase.
- Let paragraph length and sentence structure follow the amount of information. Do not repeat the same sentence pattern, add a conclusion to every paragraph, or force a fixed 起承轉合 structure.
- Do not invent experiences, emotions, quotations, dialogue, public or market reactions, or expert opinions.

## Final Checks

Confirm that every person, organization, product, date, number, causal claim, judgment, and description of certainty is supported by the material. Confirm that uncertainty and attribution remain unchanged, with no repeated sentences, vague commentary, or external knowledge. Delete anything without support.

## Output Format

Output only the rewritten article body. Keep a title if the source already has one; do not add one when the user has not requested it. Do not output analysis, fact lists, writing strategy, edit notes, check results, or additional suggestions.
